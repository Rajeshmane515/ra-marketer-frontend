Account-based marketing, commonly called ABM, takes a focused approach to B2B growth.

Instead of treating every potential customer the same, ABM focuses marketing and sales efforts on specific accounts that have strong potential value.

This approach can be particularly useful for SaaS companies with defined target markets and longer sales cycles.

Here are 10 practical ABM strategies to consider.

## 1. Define your ideal customer profile

Everything starts with selecting the right accounts.

Define the characteristics of companies that are most likely to benefit from your product.

Consider:

- Industry
- Company size
- Revenue range
- Technology stack
- Geographic market
- Business challenges
- Growth stage

A clear ideal customer profile helps marketing and sales prioritize accounts.

## 2. Build a target account list

Once you understand your ideal customer, create a list of target accounts.

You can organize accounts into tiers based on factors such as:

- Potential revenue
- Strategic importance
- Product fit
- Buying signals
- Expansion potential

This allows your team to allocate resources according to account value.

## 3. Research each account

Generic messaging rarely creates a strong ABM experience.

Research the companies you're targeting.

Look for information such as:

- Business priorities
- Recent announcements
- Hiring activity
- Technology changes
- Market expansion
- Leadership changes
- Relevant business challenges

Use this information to make your campaigns more relevant.

## 4. Identify multiple stakeholders

B2B SaaS purchases often involve more than one person.

Depending on the product, stakeholders could include:

- Executives
- Marketing leaders
- Sales leaders
- IT teams
- Finance
- Operations
- End users

Create messaging that addresses the concerns of different stakeholders.

## 5. Personalize your messaging

ABM does not always mean creating completely different content for every individual.

Start with meaningful personalization.

For example, your messaging can reference:

- Industry challenges
- Company growth
- Specific business priorities
- Relevant use cases
- Account-specific opportunities

The objective is to show that your company understands the prospect's situation.

## 6. Align sales and marketing

ABM works best when sales and marketing share responsibility for target accounts.

Both teams should agree on:

- Target accounts
- Account priorities
- Messaging
- Campaigns
- Qualification criteria
- Follow-up processes

Regular communication helps keep campaigns aligned.

## 7. Create account-specific content

Generic content can be useful, but high-value accounts may benefit from more targeted resources.

Examples include:

- Personalized landing pages
- Industry-specific guides
- Account research
- Custom presentations
- Use-case documents
- Personalized email sequences

The content should be useful rather than personalized simply for the sake of personalization.

## 8. Use multiple channels

A single channel may not be enough to reach everyone involved in an account.

An ABM campaign can combine:

- Email
- LinkedIn
- Paid advertising
- Content
- Webinars
- Sales outreach
- Retargeting

The goal is to create a consistent experience across the buying journey.

## 9. Use buying signals

Not every target account is ready to buy.

Monitor relevant signals that may indicate increased interest.

Examples can include:

- Website engagement
- Content downloads
- Product page visits
- Webinar participation
- New hiring activity
- Increased interaction with campaigns

These signals can help sales teams prioritize follow-up.

## 10. Measure account-level performance

Traditional marketing metrics alone may not provide the full picture of ABM performance.

Look at account-level metrics such as:

- Account engagement
- Meetings created
- Opportunities generated
- Pipeline created
- Opportunity conversion
- Revenue from target accounts

The objective is to understand whether your efforts are creating meaningful engagement and business outcomes.

## ABM for SaaS: Keep the customer journey in mind

ABM should not stop when an account becomes a customer.

The same account-focused approach can support:

- Expansion
- Cross-selling
- Upselling
- Customer advocacy
- Renewals

For SaaS companies, the long-term value of an account can be just as important as the initial sale.

## Conclusion

ABM works by concentrating marketing and sales resources on accounts that matter most to the business.

For SaaS companies, a strong ABM strategy combines account selection, research, personalization, sales alignment, multi-channel engagement, and account-level measurement.

The most effective ABM campaigns are not simply more personalized.

They are more relevant, more coordinated, and more focused on business outcomes.
